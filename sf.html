I’m a Washington D.C. area photographer with a fascination for photographing all things live and loud. I specialize in capturing the power and energy of a live show.

Photography isn’t my full-time gig, but I dig this stuff, and it shows up in some good places.

Shadowflood work has been featured on album covers, countless websites, in newspapers like The Washington Post, and in a few Fender and Jackson Guitars catalogs.
